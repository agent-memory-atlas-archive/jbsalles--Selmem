# GENESIS Preference Emergence 1 — exploratory diagnostic

## Goal and limitations

Test whether the existing SelMem organ (unchanged) can **form**, **transmit**, and **revise** self-representations grounded in first-person experience. Distinguish observed action patterns from an *actual preference*: a claim like "I often consult" is not itself "I like consulting". No fixture directly declares a preference to SelMem.

All histories are **scripted simulations**; they are not independently lived or actor-chosen events. Contradictory experiences are imposed, not generated by Grok. Two mirrored histories are not 96 independent participants. This experiment cannot demonstrate consciousness, creativity, or generalized individuality.

## Frozen intervention

The compiled `self-emergence.json` fixture defines five separate lives in the same `incident` context:

- `consult` / `check`: three distinct performed first steps of one action, with explicit emotional responses such as satisfaction or reassurance, but no "I prefer" assertion.
- `consult_action` / `check_action`: the corresponding successful actions **without** subjective evaluations; self-model formation control only.
- `neutral`: routine notes without performed decisions or reactions; negative formation control.

The two affect-bearing lives then receive three independent, contrary successful experiences. Immutable snapshots are written at `initial`, `after_1_counter`, and `after_3_counters` (the two action-only and neutral controls have an initial checkpoint only). Each action and its source ID is distinct. The organ's own source-specific self-consolidation guard and confidence updates remain active; no direct preferences are added and no threshold is reduced. Empty recall or absent self models are recorded, not silently filled.

For each active life and both extreme phases, two held-out tasks (the same neutral tasks as GENESIS Self-Preference) are evaluated in both option orders and six arms:

| Arm | Delivered to Grok |
| --- | --- |
| `episodes` | Organs' recalled lived episode gists only |
| `episodes_self` | Exact same gists plus the current self proposition (if one exists) |
| `self_only` | Current self proposition, no gists |
| `memory_ablation` | Episodes recalled from the same checkpoint after removing self traces in a read-only copy; no self delivery |
| `self_swap` | Same original gists plus self proposition from the opposite simulated life at the same phase |
| `explicit_control` | Direct current preference supplied as a **positive actor-sensitivity control**, not claimed as the organ's output |

Total: **2 lives × 2 actor phases × 2 tasks × 2 orders × 6 arms = 96 actor choices**; the intermediate correction snapshot is assessed structurally, not sent to Grok. Source readouts and all 96 prompts are committed *before* the first actor call. Responses are matched exactly by history, phase, task, and option order. No post-hoc task filtering or calibration gate.

## Measurement and qualitative coding

Read `checkpoints.json` and the per-checkpoint files first. Check `self_consolidation_audit`, active self propositions, source IDs, confidence, retirement, `self_natural`, `self_explicit`, and whether gists are actually present. A scorer approval is **not** proof that a preference (as distinct from a repeated action pattern) has formed: review claim and source evidence manually, preferably blinded to the experimental history label.

Use `measures.json` for matched changes, first-option bias, positive control, and within-history revision. An empty self line makes a self-injection contrast uninformative, not a successful null effect. Retention of the initial preference after contradictory evidence is not necessarily a bug; track whether confidence or retirement changes first. A working explicit-preference control only qualifies the *actor*, not the memory organ.

There are only two mirrored active lives and two highly similar tasks; treat counts as **descriptive**, not independent hypothesis tests. The production actor receives no arm name or history label. All original source checkpoints are read-only during actor collection.

```
# Apply patch and validate

bash experiments/genesis/run-self-emergence.sh experiments/genesis/results/self-emergence-mock-001 --mock

# Recommended: first collect organ formation and all frozen readouts.

bash experiments/genesis/run-self-emergence.sh experiments/genesis/results/self-emergence-choices-grok-001 --choices-from experiments/genesis/results/self-emergence-formation-grok-001

# Alternatively run formation and choices together with a fresh output.

```

Results: `design.json` (versioned fixture hash, code hash, provider info, budget); `checkpoints/*.json`, `checkpoints.json`, `snapshots/*.vault`, `actor-plan.json`; `trials.jsonl`, `responses.jsonl`, `measures.json`, `report.json`. An incomplete run is marked and preserved; `--choices-from` validates snapshot hashes and the original code/fixture/provider. Use a new output for every run; no overwrite or implicit resume.

## Decision rule

No broad success claim without (1) grounded personal evaluation beyond an action-frequency summary, (2) verified delivery in actor prompts, (3) order-balanced paired differences over episode-only and no-self controls, (4) a passing explicit-sensitivity control, and (5) interpretable longitudinal state change after contrary experience. Otherwise report the earliest observed failure in the pipeline.

## Recorded exploratory run (October 2026)

Grok 4.3 completed 70 formation HTTP attempts and 96/96 actor choices. Four
action histories (with and without reported affect) formed contextual beliefs;
the neutral control did not. Three opposing observations retired the active
initial self beliefs without forming a new opposite belief. The initial self
swap changed 6/8 matched choices, but adding own self to the same episodes
changed only 1/16. The labelled memory-ablation actor prompts were accidentally
identical to episode-only prompts; no ablation conclusion is admissible.
These are explicitly delivered self claims, not autonomous preference reversal.
See [recorded results, limitations and archive hashes](SELF_BEHAVIOR_RESULTS.md).
