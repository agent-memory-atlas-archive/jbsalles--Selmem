# Creative experiments: early indications, not proof

**The project has early qualitative indications consistent with creative recombination and memory-conditioned variation. It has not demonstrated a useful creativity advantage attributable to SelMem.** Interesting outputs are a reason to test the hypothesis; they are not evidence that the same model could not produce equally good answers without memory.

## What is published

[Compact exports](evidence/README.md) contain the exact recorded proposals and reduced audit/provenance information from four distinct interventions. Original raw run trees, caches, transport logs and checkpoint vaults stay in separate local evidence archives. No independent blind ratings are included; a prior single unblinded assistant review is not presented as a benchmark.

| Lot | Source units and treatment | Recorded work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| creative-preview-003 | Two complete histories, 788 turns, ten nights per history, five conditions, two repeats on three tasks | 60 proposals; 887 HTTP attempts including retries |
| creative-replay-003 | Frozen selections from preview-002, guarded task-blind readout and a no-memory two-step comparator | 72 proposals; 114 attempts |
| creative-replay-004 | Same accepted readouts, structured memory delivery in final generation | 72 proposals; 72 attempts |
| memory-influence-005 | Two controlled lives, six tasks; grounding reevaluation on fixed candidate pools | 72 output rows; 33 new attempts; most prior work reused |

These are not independent confirmations. Replays share inputs; the influence lot reuses 24 direct answers, 48 pools and 48 quality results. The preview and replay condition names refer to different generation/readout protocols.

## Observed mechanisms

The fresh preview completed all planned cells. Checkpoint hashes matched and post-sleep full/direct-gist comparisons used the same selected IDs. All 887 wire requests recorded reasoning effort none. Three capacity errors were overcome. Schema validity means a proposal was collected, not that its task constraints or originality were validated.

Sleep produced the following state changes:

| History | Retained traces | Latent after sleep | Axioms after sleep |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| conv-26 | 261 | 113 | 45 |
| conv-30 | 219 | 79 | 36 |

No co-recall links were recorded. The 81 axioms and reading profiles were excluded from generation in this controlled preview. It therefore cannot establish their creative contribution. Latency is not deletion and useful forgetting has not been demonstrated by these counts.

The guard accepted 43 trace retellings without a gist fallback. That is a fallible model provenance judgment, not independent evidence of factual preservation or creativity. Retelling compressed context: about 62 words after sleep, versus 144 for direct gists and 593 for RAG. The result cannot isolate retelling from that information change.

## Qualitative examples, with controls

The following are illustrative interpretations, not blinded rankings. Full texts and original problems are in [preview proposals](evidence/creative-preview-003/proposals.json).

| ID | Mechanism | Condition | Why inspect it / remaining weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C0030 | Rotate optional repair micro-actions, with an explicit stop card | Sleep + guarded retelling | Concrete participation mechanism; a parts-weight metric needs extra equipment and justification. |
| C0035 | Repeated interviews around personal artifacts, then contact exchange and a future joint activity | Sleep + guarded retelling | Links an artifact to repeated social contact; the base model may supply the same kind of idea. |
| C0045 | Replies to a story wall trigger a small meeting once a thread reaches three responses | Sleep without retelling | A plausible link from asynchronous participation to a gathering; contacting anonymous authors needs clarification. |
| C0017 | Repeated paired walks and a shared memory map | Empty context | A useful counterexample: interesting mechanisms also occur without memory. |
| C0039 | Detect deliberate drawing errors, exchange rules and redraw | Sleep + guarded retelling | Distinctive in this set, but its fit to learning a difficult practical skill is questionable. |

These examples are consistent with creative variation. They do not isolate a memory-caused mechanism, establish worldwide novelty or show average superiority over controls. Historical source dependence would require declared interventions and independent evaluation.

## Selection can change an answer without improving it

The influence diagnostic separates generation, anonymous task-only quality and anonymous grounding. On fixed pools, the moderate memory coefficient (0.5) changes 3/24 memory choices relative to zero. The same task-only model rates utility lower for two changes and equal for one. Across those three, originality changes once upward, once downward and once not at all. This is a measurable selection intervention with mixed outcomes, not a creativity win.

[Changed selections](evidence/memory-influence-005/changed-selections.json) and [selection audits](evidence/memory-influence-005/selection-audit.json) expose the tradeoffs. Pipeline model ratings are not independent scientific judges. A higher coefficient may produce more switches without better answers.

## What would strengthen the claim

1. At least two independent, condition-blind judges rating original answers for utility, mechanism originality, feasibility and concrete constraint compliance.
2. Per-history comparisons and disagreement reports, keeping generation repeats nested within their source lives.
3. A separately declared readout comparison controlling compression and information loss on the same selected traces.
4. Source-specific and sham interventions showing that a useful mechanism depends on the history rather than the base model or task cue.
5. Fresh independent histories and model replications, with negative outcomes retained.

The 600- and 6,480-proposal longitudinal plans remain available; their scale is not a substitute for a qualified measurement. [Replay instructions](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) and [protocol index](README.md) distinguish fresh replication from exact historical resumption.
